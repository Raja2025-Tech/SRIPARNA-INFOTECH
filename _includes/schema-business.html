{%- comment -%} The LocalBusiness entity, defined once for the whole site. Every page that carries structured data emits this same node with the same @id, so Google resolves one consistent business across the site rather than several near-duplicates that drifted apart. Geo coordinates are the real ones from the business's own Google Business Profile (resolved from the maps.app.goo.gl link used in the footer), not an approximation of "Barasat". "Organization" is included in @type alongside LocalBusiness rather than declared as a second top-level entity. LocalBusiness already subtypes Organization in schema.org's hierarchy, and Google's own guidance is not to mark up the same real-world business as two separate JSON-LD nodes -- that reads as two businesses, not one with two roles. Pass with_rating=true on the homepage only. aggregateRating is deliberately not repeated site-wide: the ratings are the business's own Google reviews, and Google does not use self-serving LocalBusiness review markup for rich results, so duplicating it adds risk without benefit.
