Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

At Sriparna Infotech, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or use our local hardware services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, and physical address when you voluntarily submit a service request via our website's contact form, WhatsApp, or phone call. We only collect this information to schedule on-site visits and provide physical hardware maintenance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is solely used to:

  • Respond to your service inquiries.
  • Schedule and perform local hardware diagnostics and CCTV installations.
  • Send invoices or service updates.

3. Data Protection and Device Privacy

As a local hardware service center, we respect the privacy of the data stored on your personal devices. We do not ask for remote access to your computer over the internet. Any data recovery or software configuration is done locally with your consent.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We use standard third-party tools like Google Analytics to track basic website traffic, which does not identify individual users.
